## Decision

- Recommendation: reject without repeat or OJ.
- Observed result: restoring main-kernel ST pressure did not recover performance. Case 2 was aggregate-flat with one slow and one fast arm, while case 4 regressed `1.6957%` in both arms. The extra direct-map generation/read traffic dominates any removed lookup dependency.
- Independent audit: not required; the lane adds no intra-kernel barrier/async primitive/LDS lifetime/thread mapping/sort-rank change, and ordered default-stream kernel completion is already the repository's publication mechanism.
- OJ boundary: no submission because neither prefill target supports the leaderboard path.
- Follow-up: close direct routed-row map and packed-width variants; exp-261 already bounds the smaller token-map form at only `0.9517%` case-2 gain with case-4 regression.
