## Hypothesis And Audit

- Proposed change: replace only the eight per-thread decode-m4 prologue dummy `token_ids` `ldg_b32` requests with the public last-K256-tile B prefetch address while preserving request count, volatile use, real A/B BSM requests, arrival/barrier expressions, geometry, epilogue, dispatch and ABI.
- Exact public mapping uses `g=tidx/16`, `l=tidx%16`, `stage=0..3`, `ldgi=0..1`, `n=min(4*g+stage+64*ldgi,col_limit-1)` and `k=16*(l^g)`. It samples 8,192 unique B bytes spanning every cache line of the 32-KiB last B tile before the normal BSM prologue reloads that tile.
- No bytes, BSM requests, LDS operations or MMA work are eliminated. Even granting a zero-cost prefetch and a completely free last B tile, the A+B traffic-share ceiling is at most `1/(2T)`: `1.79%` for case 1 (`T=28`) and `6.25%` for case 3 (`T=8`). These are below the approximately `5.27%` and `10.55%` reductions required for the next displayed points.
- The mechanism only touches decode cases 1/3 and therefore cannot supply the three additional raw case points required for score `85.5` even under its impossible upper bound.
- Real behavior is less favorable: the original requests target a tiny hot token working set, while the replacement starts cold B cache-line fetches, can lengthen the prologue gvm wait and competes with the immediately following BSM requests. Importing the public file wholesale would also inherit already rejected asynchronous A behavior; no such import is allowed.
