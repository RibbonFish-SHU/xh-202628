## Hypothesis And Change

Remove the eight prologue-only dummy `token_ids` loads from the decode m4
kernel and use the public `nodummy_waitfix` arrival thresholds: `4`, then `0`.
The steady loop and drain arrival sequence remain unchanged.

## Audit

- Exact-commit synchronization audit passed for C500 testing. The prologue
  issues 16 FIFO BSM requests. Waiting at 4 retires the first 12 before stage-0
  LDS; waiting at GVM/BSM zero publishes all requests before stage-1 LDS. The
  first steady iteration restores the formal eight-request carry invariant.
- Two independent provenance/performance audits found no benchmark or OJ result
  for the public waitfix. It exists only in a bulk personal-branch snapshot and
  was not selected by that branch's active wrapper or final OJ sources.
- The waits are conservative: `4/0` drains stages 2/3 before the first MMA loop,
  while the formal `12/8` sequence overlaps them.
- Case 1 removes only 32,768 one-time wave instructions out of approximately
  52,862,976 (`0.06198%`). The maximum lane-byte reduction is 8,388,608 bytes,
  `0.8595%` of profiled global reads, and the 16 KiB token working set is hot.
  This is far below the `5.27%` latency reduction required for the next point.

## Decision

Reject before C500 and OJ. The exact patch is synchronization-safe enough to
test but has no credible leaderboard-tier path and can reduce prologue overlap.
No controller candidate was enqueued and the formal-best source was untouched.
