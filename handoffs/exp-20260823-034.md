## Decision

- Observed result: committed protected-source proof, CUDA build, correctness, benchmark and regression all passed; raw status is `succeeded` with exit code 0. The candidate halves LDS and removes all A shared operations at unchanged logical bytes, but no target performance conclusion is available.
- Risks or unresolved target-only behavior: MXCC must accept indexing a native four-int vector held in a two-dimensional array and lower its direct b128 assignments without spills or hidden copies. More importantly, direct loads scatter each instruction across 16 rows: the aligned 128-byte full-wave transaction model doubles from 128 to 256, and a 16-lane coalescer could increase requests to 1,024 at every modeled segment size. Public A allocations, row strides and K-tile offsets are expected to be 128-byte aligned, but only target ISA/runtime evidence can establish the actual C500 coalescing granularity and whether the 16-KiB LDS/register savings outweigh transaction pressure.
