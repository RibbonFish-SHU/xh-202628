## Decision

- Observed result: reject. Although sampled correctness passed, candidate arm A inflated the modified decode case 1 to `11.014 ms` and then inflated source-identical prefill case 2 to `40.202 ms`; arm B returned both to baseline-like timings. This severe order-dependent behavior is incompatible with a stable OJ submission and is consistent with asynchronous queue work not being safely drained across launches.
- Risks: A-only async may still create an unsafe or exhausted DMA queue; any correctness/runtime failure rejects the candidate. Even a correct small gain may be below the approximately `5.27%` case-1 and `10.57%` case-3 next-point requirements.
- Historical denylist checked: differs from exp-126/127 K128 B-only async single-bank failures, exp-047 K128 compiler-managed BSM WA, exp-199 all-sync m4, exp-209 predicate cleanup and exp-217 M64 residency. The public fully async A+B m4 queue failures are not replayed because B remains synchronous.
- Recommendation: reject without repeat, integration or OJ; close A-only asynchronous m4 under the inherited arrival schedule.
- Independent audit required: no promotion remains. The exact local inverse and C500 correctness establish implementation identity, while the decisive ABBA instability rejects the mechanism before OJ.
- Suggested next action: record and enqueue the immutable rejection, keep the formal source exact, and investigate a non-async structural mechanism.
