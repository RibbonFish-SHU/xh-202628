## Decision

- Observed result: exact source/job provenance, target builds, 3/3 correctness, full regression, sampled correctness, unchanged resource ceilings, source/tree integrity and manifest verification all pass. MXCC retains a real packed-MUL IR distinction, but target cases improve by only `0.3549%` and `0.0144%`, both arms miss `1%`, and noisy case 1 regresses `2.6066%`; the immutable candidate fails the performance gate
- Risks or unresolved OJ-environment behavior: signed-zero bits can differ for exact negative-zero products even though the official tolerance accepts the values. C500-local timing is not an OJ score prediction, but the target deltas are far below the predeclared gate and unchanged resources/binary size provide no basis for a repeat
- Historical denylist checked: no native int-to-float vector spelling, scale-pair streaming, BF16 intrinsic, specialization, address widening, BSM, K64, direct-global-A, accumulator seeding, row interleave, mapping, barrier, NVIDIA or second mechanism
- Recommendation: reject
- Independent audit required: no; this is a documented arithmetic intrinsic substitution with exact inverse and unchanged mapping/synchronization traces
- Suggested next action: finalize exp-119 as a C500-local rejection without repeat, integration or OJ; the packed-FP32-MUL-for-zero-FMA mechanism family is closed on this baseline
