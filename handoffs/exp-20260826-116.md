## Decision

- Observed result: the compile-safe native-vector array spelling passes trusted build, 3/3 differential correctness, complete regression, sampled correctness, integrity, manifest and resource gates, but changes no reported resource and fails the precommitted performance gate: cases 2-4 are flat below `0.1%`, and case 1 regresses `1.4643%` in aggregate with conflicting arms. There is no evidence that the intrinsic improves this kernel
- Risks or unresolved OJ-environment behavior: C500 evidence is paired-local and does not predict absolute OJ timing. Baseline bit-add has unusual NaN payload behavior not exhaustively covered by the three differential cases, but the candidate is already rejected on performance and will not reach OJ. The whole-array store view and direct intrinsic spelling are target-compiler valid; the remaining risk is lack of benefit, not source validity
- Historical denylist checked: no vector-element address, direct truncation, ties-away rounding, native int-to-float vector spelling, pair-register/scale streaming, store widening, specialization, BSM, K64, direct-global-A, cluster/map/grid or synchronization change; no NVIDIA, C500, OJ, browser, controller, main, GitHub or formal-state access
- Recommendation: reject
- Independent audit required: no; exact provenance/coverage and target build/correctness are proven, while the performance failure is direct and stable on three of four cases
