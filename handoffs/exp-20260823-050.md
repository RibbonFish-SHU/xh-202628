## Decision

- Observed result: both independent stop conditions fired before editing source: no documented predicated b128 global-store intrinsic is available, and the two current b64 stores owned by one thread are 128 bytes apart rather than adjacent. The proposed one-for-two replacement therefore cannot be expressed as assigned.
- Risks or unresolved target-only behavior: a newer target SDK could contain an undocumented store builtin, but inventing or probing a spelling is disallowed and would still not solve the independent nonadjacent value ownership. Any viable widening would require a separately scoped cross-lane exchange or output remap plus target compiler and correctness validation.
- Historical denylist checked: no guessed intrinsic, cross-lane transfer, output remap, predicate removal, unpredicated tail store, alternate thread mapping, tile change, BSM, asynchronous synchronization or second mechanism was implemented.
- Recommendation: reject
- Independent audit required: no; no source, synchronization, asynchronous intrinsic, LDS reuse or thread mapping change exists to audit.
- Suggested next action: close this mechanism family. Reopen only if a documented predicated b128 store ABI is supplied and a separate lane explicitly authorizes and proves the required value-owner remapping.
